Terms of service

This is a starter template. Have it reviewed by a lawyer and adapt it to your company before launch.

1. The service. RepairStop connects vehicle owners with independent, verified mechanics and garages. Mechanics are independent partners, not employees of RepairStop.

2. Pricing. You pay a visit fee shown before you book. After inspecting, the mechanic sends a repair estimate that you may approve or decline. Declining means you pay only the visit fee.

3. Accounts. You must provide accurate information and keep your password confidential. RepairStop does not verify email ownership. Share your start code only with the mechanic who is physically with you.

4. Cancellations. You may cancel until the mechanic arrives. Repeated misuse may lead to suspension.

5. Payments. Payments are made online through our payment partner or in cash to the mechanic, who confirms receipt.

6. Reviews. Only customers with a completed booking can post reviews. We may hide reviews that break our rules.

7. Safety. The SOS feature alerts your trusted contact by email. It does not replace emergency services; call 112 in danger.

8. Liability. To the extent permitted by law, RepairStop is not liable for indirect losses arising from work done by independent mechanics.
