Questions

Frequently asked questions

How does RepairStop work?

Share your location and problem, choose a mechanic, track him live, approve the final quote after inspection and pay online or in cash.

What do I pay first?

Only a visit fee. The repair cost is confirmed after the mechanic finds the actual problem, and no work starts until you approve.

What if I decline the repair?

You pay the visit fee only. You can also ask for a tow to a garage.

How is my safety protected?

Mechanics are verified, you share an OTP at the start of the job, and the SOS button alerts your trusted contact.

How can I join as a mechanic?

Tap For mechanics, register with your documents and, once verified, go online to receive nearby jobs.
