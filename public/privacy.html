Privacy policy

This is a starter template. Have it reviewed by a lawyer and adapt it to your company before launch.

What we collect. Account details (name, email, phone), booking details (location, vehicle, service), partner documents, payment status, reviews, and an optional trusted contact.

How we use it. To run bookings, verify partners, send service emails (verification, password reset, SOS), process payments and keep the platform safe.

Who sees it. A mechanic sees your exact location and phone number only after accepting your booking. Public reviews show only your first name. Partner documents are visible only to administrators.

Security. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. Sessions use secure cookies. Payment card data is handled by our payment provider and never stored by us.

Location. Your browser asks permission before sharing GPS. Address search and nearby police results use OpenStreetMap services.

Your choices. You can edit your profile any time and contact us to delete your account.
